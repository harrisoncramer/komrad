Komrad's triage section holds a short list of implementer agents that need your attention. It is a zone at the top of the agents list that holds only the agents waiting on you, you can work through them instead of scanning the whole tree.

{/* recording: the triage zone filling as agents ask questions and finish */}

The zone holds three implementer agents by default, which can be configured in the <Setting path="layout.triage_rows" /> setting.

## What lands in triage

An agent enters triage for one of three reasons:

1. The agent needs you. The agent stopped to ask a question or ask for permission, for instance.
2. Something broke. The agent errored, failed to launch, failed to clean up, a hook script failed, etc.
3. It's turn finished, no reviewers reviewing it's changes, and it's not waiting on any messages from other agents. It's fully idle, in other words.

## Working through triage

Press <Key name="agents.triage" /> to jump to the first agent in the zone. The agents in the zone are copies, so each one still sits in its usual place in the tree below. While the cursor is in the zone its order holds still, so nothing moves under you as you read. It settles into its new order once you leave.

Answer the agent and it drops out of triage on its own. Opening a finished agent counts as having looked at it, so it leaves the zone too.

When you can't deal with a family yet, open the [menu](../navigation/#the-agent-menu) with <Key name="menu.open" /> and press <Key name="menu.snooze" />. The family leaves the zone, a family waiting for a slot comes in at the bottom, and the cursor moves to the family above it. A snooze lasts until what the family waits on changes, so an agent you snoozed for finishing comes back when it asks you something.

To bring a snoozed family back early, find it in the tree and pick wake from the same menu. Snoozes only last as long as the panel does, so restarting the panel brings every snoozed family back.
