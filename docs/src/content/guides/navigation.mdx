Komrad's panel has a few sections: the sidebar, the info panel, and the main panel. The view controls what each section shows.

## The sidebar

The sidebar typically lists the resource you're looking at, such as workflows and their tasks, or worktrees.

The view switcher at the top of the sidebar moves between these subsections. Press <Key name="views.next" /> or <Key name="views.previous" /> to open the next or previous view.

The details pane underneath shows what komrad knows about the active row. For a task, it shows the task's directory, branch and pull request.

Finally, the help menu at the bottom of every sidebar lists the available hotkeys. Press <Key name="help.toggle" /> to bring up the help.

## The main view

The main view is the wide region beside the sidebar. On the agents view, it shows the agent you have opened.

To move between the sidebar and the main view, press komrad's leader key twice: `ctrl+space`. Komrad also has "jump mode" which lets you jump to any of the different sections on the screen. Press `ctrl+space j` to enter jump mode, which draws a big letter over each section. Komrad also has visual mode, for selecting text, and edit mode, for when you use vim keybindings. The bottom right of the sidebar names your current mode.

To resize the section you're in, press `ctrl+space h` to push its edge left or `ctrl+space l` to push it right. Pressing `h` or `l` again right away takes another step without the leader. On the review, the diff sits between the list and the agent, so `h` widens the diff into the list and `l` widens it into the agent.

## The menu

Most actions live in the menu. Press <Key name="menu.open" /> and it lists what you can do with the row under the cursor, over the screen's own entries. Each entry names its key, and an entry that opens a further menu, such as git or archive, opens it to the right.

The row you hover decides what the menu holds.

## Navigation, Sorting, and Filtering

Komrad's keybindings are heavily inspired by Vim.

The <Key name="nav.down" /> and <Key name="nav.up" /> keys move the cursor a row, <Key name="nav.page_down" /> and <Key name="nav.page_up" /> move it a chunk of rows, and `gg` and <Key name="nav.bottom" /> jump to the first and last row. The mouse wheel scrolls whichever region it is over and hands it the keys, as if you had jumped there.

Press `/` to search. Typing lights up the rows that match, and `enter` puts the cursor on the nearest one. Use the <Key name="filter.next_match" /> and <Key name="filter.prev_match" /> key to then step through the matches. Lower case text ignores case, and searches support regular expressions.

You can also use the search in "filter" mode. While typing, press Tab to turn it into a filter, which takes every row that does not match off the screen instead of stepping through them.

Press <Key name="tabs.focus" /> to reach the sort strip over the list, where you pick the column to sort by. Its last stop groups the rows under their workflows. Flattening them makes the list easier to sort by activity or name.

## Notifications

When komrad reports something, such as an error, its summary flashes for a few seconds at the top of the sidebar. Press <Key name="messages.open" /> to read every message in the main view, where <Key name="messages.dismiss" /> dismisses the one under the cursor and <Key name="messages.dismiss_all" /> dismisses them all.

## Visual selection

Press <Key name="selection.toggle" /> to take the row under the cursor into a selection, and <Key name="selection.range" /> to take in every row between the cursor and the nearest selected one. An action that works on one task then works on every chosen task, such as hiding or archiving them, or restoring and deleting them from the archive.

## The details

The details under the list show more about the row under the cursor, such as a task's directory, branch and pull request. Press `ctrl+space j` and the letter over them to move the keyboard there, and press <Key name="details.copy" /> to copy the value of the field under the cursor to your clipboard. `/` searches the fields the same way it searches the list.
