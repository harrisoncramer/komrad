Komrad's panel is divided up into a few sections: the sidebar, the info panel, and the main panel. These different sections hold differnet things, depending on the view.

## The sidebar

The sidebar typically lists the resource you're looking at, such as agents or worktrees.

The view switcher at the top of the sidebar moves between these subsections. Press <Key name="views.next" /> or <Key name="views.previous" /> to open the next or previous view.

The details pane underneath shows what is known about the currently active row. For instance, for an agent, it might show the agent's directory and it's base branch.

Finally, the help menu at the bottom of every sidebar shows the available hotkeys. Press the `?` key to bring up the help.

## The main view

The main view is the wide region beside the sidebar. In the agent list, it shows the agent you have opened.

To move between the sidebar and the main view, press komrad's leader key twice: `ctrl+space`. Komrad also has "jump mode" which lets you jump to any of the different sections on the screen. Press the leader key, followed by `j` to enter jump mode: a big letter is drawn over each section. Komrad also has visual mode (for selecting text) and edit mode (when you are using vim keybindings). The bottom right of the sidebar names the mode you are in.

## Navigation, Sorting, and Filtering

Komrad's keybindings are heavily inspired by Vim.

The <Key name="nav.down" /> and <Key name="nav.up" /> keys move the cursor a row, <Key name="nav.page_down" /> and <Key name="nav.page_up" /> move it a chunk of rows, and `gg` and <Key name="nav.bottom" /> jump to the first and last row. The mouse wheel scrolls whichever region it is over.

Press `/` to search. Typing lights up the rows that match, and `enter` puts the cursor on the nearest one. Use the <Key name="filter.next_match" /> and <Key name="filter.prev_match" /> key to then step through the matches. Lower case text ignores case; searching supports regular expressions.

You can also use the search in "filter" mode. While typing, press Tab to turn it into a filter, which takes every row that does not match off the screen instead of stepping through them.

Press <Key name="tabs.focus" /> to reach the sort strip over the list, where you pick the column to sort by. Its last stop groups the rows under their workflows, and flattening them makes the list easier to sort by activity or name.

## Notifications

When komrad reports something, such as an error, its summary flashes for a few seconds at the top of the sidebar. Press <Key name="messages.open" /> to read every message in the main view, where <Key name="messages.dismiss" /> dismisses the one under the cursor and <Key name="messages.dismiss_all" /> dismisses them all.

Menus nest. Press <Key name="menu.git" /> in the agent menu to open the <Setting path="keybindings.git">git menu</Setting> to its right, for rebasing the agent's branch with <Key name="git.rebase" />, opening its pull request with <Key name="git.open_pull_request" />, copying its address with <Key name="git.copy_pull_request_url" />, toggling its draft with <Key name="git.toggle_draft" />, or merging it on the forge with <Key name="git.merge" />. That opens the <Setting path="keybindings.merge">merge menu</Setting>, which merges with <Key name="merge.merge" /> or merges and then archives the agent with <Key name="merge.merge_archive" />. The menu it came from stays on screen in gray, and going back returns to it.

## Visual selection

Press <Key name="selection.toggle" /> to take the row under the cursor into a selection, and <Key name="selection.range" /> to take in every row between the cursor and the nearest selected one. An action that works on one agent then works on all of them, such as archiving, or restoring and deleting from the archived agents' menu.

## The details

The details under the list show more about the row under the cursor, such as an agent's directory, branch and pull request. Press `ctrl+space j` and the letter over them to move the keyboard there, and press <Key name="details.copy" /> to copy the value of the field under the cursor to your clipboard. `/` searches the fields the same way it searches the list.
