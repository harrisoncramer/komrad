## The sidebar elements

The sidebar panel is divided into a few regions. It most of komrad's state, typically a list of whatever is in focus: agents and workflows, schedules, worktrees, or documents.

The _view switcher_ across the top of the sidebar moves between the agents, the query, the schedules, the archive and the worktrees. Press <Key name="views.next" /> or <Key name="views.previous" /> to open the next or previous view, and keep pressing them to step through the rest.

Under the sidebar is the **details pane**, which shows what is known about the row under the cursor, such as an agent's directory or its base branch.

Under that, the **help menu** shows hotkeys that work from your current cursor position.

## The main view

The main view is the wide region beside the sidebar. It shows the agent you have opened, its diff on the review screen, or any other content that's focused at the moment.

Press <Key name="cycle.views" at={0} /> to move the keyboard between regions in komrad, and <Key name="cycle.views" at={1} /> to the previous one: the list, the agent on the stage, then the details. If your own tmux prefix is <Key name="cycle.views" at={0} />, choose another <Setting path="keybindings.cycle.views">pair of cycle keys</Setting>.

When komrad reports something, such as an error, its summary flashes for a few seconds on the engine line at the top of the sidebar. Errors and warnings you have not dismissed are counted there once the flash is over. Press <Key name="messages.open" /> to read every message in the main view, where <Key name="messages.dismiss" /> dismisses the one under the cursor and <Key name="messages.dismiss_all" /> dismisses them all.

## Navigation, Sorting, and Filtering

Komrad's keybindings are heavily inspired by Vim.

The <Key name="nav.down" /> and <Key name="nav.up" /> keys move the cursor a row, <Key name="nav.page_down" /> and <Key name="nav.page_up" /> move it a chunk of rows, and `gg` and <Key name="nav.bottom" /> jump to the first and last row. The mouse wheel scrolls whichever region it is over.

Press `/` to search. Typing lights up the rows that match, and <Key name="agents.open" /> puts the cursor on the nearest one. Use the <Key name="filter.next_match" /> and <Key name="filter.prev_match" /> key to then step through the matches. Lower case text ignores case; searching supports regular expressions.

You can also use the search in "filter" mode. While typing, press Tab to turn it into a filter, which takes every row that does not match off the screen instead of stepping through them.

Press <Key name="tabs.focus" /> to reach the sort strip over the list, where you pick the column to sort by. Its last stop groups the rows under their workflows, and flattening them makes the list easier to sort by activity or name.

## The agent menu

Some screens belong to one agent rather than to a whole view. An agent has the review of its work, the documents it has written, its logs, and a terminal in its directory. Press <Key name="menu.open" /> on an agent to open its <Setting path="keybindings.menu">menu</Setting> and pick one. Leaving it takes you back to the list you came from.

Menus nest. Press <Key name="menu.git" /> in the agent menu to open the <Setting path="keybindings.git">git menu</Setting> to its right, for rebasing the agent's branch with <Key name="git.rebase" />, opening its pull request with <Key name="git.open_pull_request" />, copying its address with <Key name="git.copy_pull_request_url" />, or merging it on the forge with <Key name="git.merge" />. That opens the <Setting path="keybindings.merge">merge menu</Setting>, which merges with <Key name="merge.merge" /> or merges and then archives the agent with <Key name="merge.merge_archive" />. The menu it came from stays on screen in gray, and going back returns to it.

## Visual selection

Press <Key name="selection.toggle" /> to take the row under the cursor into a selection, and <Key name="selection.range" /> to take in every row between the cursor and the nearest selected one. An action that works on one agent then works on all of them, such as archiving, or restoring and deleting on the archive screen.

## The details

The details under the list show more about the row under the cursor, such as an agent's directory, branch, pull request and recent commits. Press <Key name="cycle.views" at={0} /> until the keyboard reaches them, and press <Key name="details.copy" /> to copy the value of the field under the cursor to your clipboard. `/` searches the fields the same way it searches the list.
